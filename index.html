What is EB-1A

A direct path to a US Green Card — built on what you have already done.

EB-1A is a US immigration category for individuals with strong, documented achievements in their field. There is no employer sponsorship, no labor certification, and no job offer requirement.

01

Self-petitioned

You file on your own behalf. No US employer required.

02

One of the fastest paths

Among the most efficient routes to a Green Card available to non-US nationals.

03

Direct permanent residency

10-year Green Card. Eligibility for US citizenship after 5 years.

04

Full family coverage

Spouse and unmarried children under 21 receive Green Cards alongside you.

Who qualifies

Built for those who have already proven themselves.

EB-1A is for accomplished professionals — not the famous. Quiet achievers with a strong record qualify just as well as public figures.

01

Business Owners

Founders and operators of companies with measurable impact and recognition in their industry.

02

Founders

Tech and consumer founders who raised capital, built products, or generated meaningful traction.

03

IT Specialists

Engineers, architects, and technical leaders with influence — open source, patents, or systems at scale.

04

Executives

C-suite, senior directors, and division heads whose work has shaped companies and outcomes.

05

Experts & Consultants

Recognized authorities, judges of others' work, speakers, authors, and high-fee advisors.

No — awards are one possible criterion, not a requirement. EB-1A is built on a set of ten possible categories, and a successful petition typically demonstrates strength in three or more. Awards are useful when present, but founders, executives, and technical leaders frequently qualify through other categories: original contributions of major significance, leading roles in distinguished organizations, judging the work of others, high salary, authorship, and media coverage.
